Meet the Doctor

Dr. Nikhil Adhao

Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon • Dedicated to Precision, Mobility & Patient Well-being

Dr. Nikhil Adhao is an accomplished Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon with over 15 years of clinical and surgical expertise. Known for his compassionate approach and clinical precision, he has successfully treated thousands of patients suffering from complex joint, bone, and spine ailments.

Dr. Adhao leads the orthopaedic practice at Chiranjivi Hospital in Mahal, Nagpur, alongside consultations at Civil Lines. His practice is equipped with modern diagnostics, specialized joint rehabilitation, and 24/7 emergency fracture trauma support.
